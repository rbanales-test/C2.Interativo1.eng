Quick Tutorial

Welcome to the Harmonic Spectrum Visualizer. Follow these simple steps to explore how harmonics shape the sound you hear:

  • Step 1: Start the Audio Engine Click the Start Audio Engine button at the top left. Make sure your device volume is set to a comfortable level. You will hear a steady tone representing a fundamental frequency.
  • Step 2: Adjust the Base Pitch Use the Fundamental Frequency slider to change the base pitch from 100 Hz to 1000 Hz. Notice how the visual wave on the Oscilloscope speeds up or slows down while the main bar on the Spectrum Analyzer shifts position.
  • Step 3: Morph Harmonic Content (Saxophone vs Clarinet) Move the Odd versus Even Harmonic Balance slider. When shifted all the way to Full Series, you hear a rich timbre containing all integer harmonics, similar to a saxophone. When shifted to Odd Harmonics Only, even-numbered bars vanish from the spectrum analyzer, producing a hollow tone similar to a clarinet or square wave.
  • Step 4: Test Inharmonicity Toggle the Inharmonicity button on. Notice how the upper partials shift away from whole numbers, instantly changing the warm tone into a metallic, bell-like timbre.
  • Step 5: Mute or Stop Audio Click Stop Audio Engine at any time to pause sound output. You can click Start Audio Engine again whenever you are ready to resume exploring.
